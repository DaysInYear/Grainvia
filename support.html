English

Getting started

Grainvia requires iOS 17 or later. Tap Edit to choose your own photo, or open an example, compare its before-and-after result and choose Use Preset. Adjust color and textures, explore subject layers, and add text only when you want it. Save or share the finished image.

Why can’t I save to Photos?

Allow Grainvia to add photos when iOS asks. If permission was denied, review Grainvia’s Photos permission in iOS Settings. Check available device storage and try again. Sharing through the system share sheet is another option.

Why is a person not separated cleanly?

Segmentation runs on the device. Fine hair, translucent fabric, motion blur and backgrounds with similar colors can make the edge less precise. Try a clearer photo with separation between the person and background, or use a composition that does not require subject separation. Original photos are not overwritten by the editing preview.

Why does Pro appear locked after I paid?

Confirm that Media & Purchases uses the Apple Account used for the purchase, connect to the internet, and choose Restore Purchases on the membership screen. Check whether the subscription is active. If the issue remains, email the app version, iOS version and error message. Do not send your password or full payment details.

How do I cancel, change a plan or request a refund?

Go to iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. See Apple’s subscription help or refund help. Deleting Grainvia does not cancel a subscription. Buying lifetime access does not cancel an existing subscription either; cancel it separately.

What should I include in a bug report?

  • Grainvia version and build, device model and iOS version.
  • The steps that led to the problem, expected result and actual result.
  • The displayed error, and an optional screenshot with personal details hidden.
  • Diagnostic logs only if you choose to export and share them. Please avoid sending private photos unless essential and you are comfortable doing so.

How can I remove my data?

Grainvia has no app account to delete. Use iOS Delete App to remove its local drafts and data; Offload App keeps app data. Remove exported photos and device backups separately if desired. Contact us to request deletion of support correspondence. More detail is in the Privacy Policy.

简体中文

如何开始编辑？

Grainvia 需要 iOS 17 或更新版本。点击“编辑”选择自己的照片；也可以打开示例，查看处理前后对比，再点击“使用预设”。随后调整色彩与质感、尝试人物层次；需要时再加入文字，最后保存或分享成片。

为什么无法保存到相册？

iOS 询问时，请允许 Grainvia 添加照片。如果此前拒绝了权限，可在 iOS 设置中检查 Grainvia 的照片权限，同时确认设备剩余存储空间。也可以通过系统分享面板导出。

人物边缘为什么不够准确？

人物分割在设备上运行。细碎发丝、半透明衣物、运动模糊和相近颜色的背景可能影响边缘效果。可尝试主体与背景更分明的照片，或选择不依赖人物分割的构图。编辑预览不会覆盖原图。

已购买却没有解锁 Pro？

确认“媒体与购买项目”使用的是原购买 Apple 账户，联网后在会员页点击“恢复购买”，并检查订阅是否仍有效。如果仍有问题，请通过邮件提供 App 版本、iOS 版本和错误提示，不要发送密码或完整付款资料。

如何取消订阅、更换周期或申请退款？

前往 iOS“设置 → 你的姓名 → 订阅”进行管理，也可查看 Apple 订阅帮助及退款帮助。删除 Grainvia 不会取消订阅；购买永久会员也不会自动取消已有订阅，需要单独操作。

反馈问题时需要提供什么？

  • Grainvia 版本和构建号、设备型号、iOS 版本。
  • 出现问题的操作步骤、预期结果与实际结果。
  • 错误提示；可选提供已遮挡个人信息的截图。
  • 仅在你主动选择时导出并分享诊断日志。除非排查确有必要且你愿意提供，否则无需发送私人照片。

如何删除我的数据？

Grainvia 没有需要注销的 App 账户。iOS 的“删除 App”会移除本地草稿与 App 数据，“卸载 App／Offload App”会保留数据。已导出照片与设备备份需要分别管理。若需删除你发给我们的支持通信，可通过邮箱联系我们。详情见隐私政策。
